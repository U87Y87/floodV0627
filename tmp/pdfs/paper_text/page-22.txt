4.3.1. Data and environment
In the experiments, the raster data in GeoCube included data from the Landsat-8
Operational Land Imager sensor with a 30 m resolution, Gaofen 1 Panchromatic &
Multispectral Scanners with a resolution of 2/8 m, and Wide-Field-of-View sensor with
a resolution of 16 m. The vector data in the GeoCube were acquired from OSM.
