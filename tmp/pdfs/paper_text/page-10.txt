3.1.1. Product dimension
This dimension specifies the thematic axis for the geospatial data cube using the
product name (e.g. Landsat_8_ARD or OSM_Water), type (e.g. raster, vector or tabular),
processes, and instrument name. The product and instrument name, are linked to the
Product&Band and Instrument tables, respectively, which are used as supplementary
tables when consuming raster data. For example, the product dimension can describe
optical and SAR image products by recording information on the instrument and
band. The supplementary tables are not necessary when vector data products such as
OpenStreetMap (OSM) are considered. The processes are the functions that have been
supported and can be performed on the product. Data querying or subsetting can be
performed along the product dimension using the product name.
3.1.2. Spatial dimension
This dimension specifies the spatial axis for the geospatial data cube using the grid
code, grid type, city name, and province name. The cube uses a spatial grid for tiling
to enable data readiness in a high-performance form. The tiling for raster data involves
